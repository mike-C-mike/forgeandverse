# Believe in the Badge Production Preflight

## Current state

The 3:2 composition is coherent enough to define real physical targets, but the current raster backdrop master is not sufficient for a premium large-format print.

Current source:

- `source-assets/work/believe-in-the-badge-backdrop.png`
- 1536 x 1024 pixels

At the working 36 x 24 inch metal target, that source provides only about 42.7 pixels per inch before crop or recomposition.

Do not upscale the current file and call it production-ready.

## Working physical targets

### Floating metal

- working trim: 36 x 24 inches
- intended setting: briefing rooms and shared professional workspaces
- finish direction: low-gloss or matte with deep blacks and restrained reflection
- mount: concealed float mount with deliberate wall separation

### Archival paper and frame

- working trim: 30 x 20 inches
- intended setting: personal offices and quieter reading-led spaces
- finish direction: archival paper with controlled contrast and low glare
- frame: substantial dark frame with the mat treated as part of the composition

## Working artwork requirements

For the 36 x 24 target:

- photographic master: at least 5400 x 3600 pixels for 150 ppi at final size
- preferred when source quality supports it: 7200 x 4800 pixels or higher
- typography and linework should remain vector where possible
- final color and export settings must follow the selected production partner's profile/template

## Working geometry before a vendor template exists

- preserve the 3:2 aspect ratio
- use 0.25 inch working bleed outside trim
- keep critical text at least 0.75 inch inside trim
- vendor specifications override these temporary values

## Physical sample test

The sample must prove:

- full copy readable from roughly 3-5 feet
- title and overall composition recognizable across the room
- deep blacks retain detail under normal office light
- reflections remain controlled under overhead and oblique light
- image supports rather than overwhelms the writing
- edge depth and wall separation feel intentional from the side
- metal and framed paper are compared in the same room before a primary edition is selected

## Production gate

Do not order the physical sample until a higher-resolution, rights-cleared master exists.
