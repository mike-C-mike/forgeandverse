# Rights Checklist

Complete this review before a work becomes downloadable or receives an external-edition link.

## Source review

- Is the writing original to Forge & Verse?
- Are all photographs and illustrations original, licensed, commissioned, or clearly permitted?
- Does the work quote another source?
- Is the quoted text public domain in the relevant jurisdiction?
- Does the design borrow a recognizable modern edition, layout, photograph, or adaptation?
- Are agency patches, seals, uniforms, trademarks, or vendor marks visible?
- Does a mockup contain stock material with restrictions on merchandise or resale?

## Release classification

Use one internal value:

- `original`
- `original-free-release`
- `public-domain-inspired`
- `licensed`
- `commentary-only`
- `display-only`

## Final gate

A work marked `commentary-only` or `display-only` should not receive a download file or external purchase URL.

When the rights position is unclear, keep the work inside the studio. A blank button is cheaper than a confident mistake.
