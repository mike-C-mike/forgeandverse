# Prototype Testing

A Forge & Verse study advances because it survives the moment it was designed for, not because the mockup looks finished.

## Bench Status Pad 0.3

### Print

Use `source-assets/prototypes/bench-status-pad-prototype-letter-2up.pdf` and print in landscape at 100 percent / Actual Size. Cut one proof to 4 x 6 inches.

### Test

1. Choose a realistic active-case scenario.
2. Complete the sheet during the final five minutes of a work session.
3. Mark Process running, Do not interrupt, or Blocked only when that state changes what the returning examiner should do.
4. Put the sheet away for at least two hours; overnight or a weekend is better.
5. Return without reopening full case notes first.
6. Start a timer and state the current process, last completed action, next action, and open loop.
7. Record the time and mark any field that was ignored, cramped, ambiguous, or duplicative.
8. Securely destroy the test sheet if real case information was used and policy permits destruction.

### Pass condition

The examiner recovers the active case state accurately in under thirty seconds without needing to decode the sheet.

## Examination Notebook 0.3

### Print

Use `source-assets/prototypes/examination-notebook-interior-prototype.pdf`. The current proof is 32 pages at 5.5 x 8.5 inches and is intended for physical workflow testing, not approved case use.

### Test

1. Print double-sided at 100 percent or inspect at exact trim size if half-letter duplex printing is unavailable.
2. Hold, clip, or temporarily bind the stack so the mirrored gutter can be judged as a real inner edge.
3. Use the structured pages only where they help; skipped fields/pages are useful evidence.
4. Spend at least twenty minutes each on ruled, ruled-with-margin, dot-grid, and open page treatments.
5. Use at least three ordinary pens.
6. Record bleed-through, feathering, dry time, tooth, hand comfort, and show-through.
7. Decide whether a mixed interior is useful or whether one ruling style should dominate a production edition.
8. Confirm how handwritten examination notes are treated by local policy and discovery practice before any real-case pilot.

### Pass condition

The five structured pages orient the work without duplicating software, the gutter remains comfortable when treated as bound, and the open pages remain the reason to carry the notebook.

## Advancing a study

Record:

- what was tested
- what changed
- what was removed
- what remains unresolved
- whether the release test passed

Do not advance a study because time has passed or because a category looks empty.
