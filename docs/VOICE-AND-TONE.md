# Voice and Tone

## Core voice

Forge & Verse is literate, restrained, practitioner-aware, and capable of a sharp edge without becoming theatrical.

The work should feel:

- specific rather than generic
- confident rather than loud
- sincere without becoming sentimental sludge
- technically credible without reading like documentation
- occasionally funny in a way the intended room understands

## Public use of “eloquent badass”

The phrase may appear when it lands naturally. It is not a footer slogan, permanent badge, or requirement for every page. The site should usually demonstrate the idea rather than announce it.

## Humor

Humor comes from affection for the profession and recognition of shared experience. It should never depend on contempt for the person doing the job.

A good insider joke rewards knowledge. A weak one merely puts a job title on a mug.

## Writing checks

Before publishing, ask:

1. Could this line belong to almost any profession?
2. Does the detail prove someone understands the work?
3. Is the language carrying weight, or merely wearing work boots?
4. Does the joke respect the person it is about?
5. Would the piece still be worth making if it never became a physical edition?
