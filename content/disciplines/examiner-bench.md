The evidence may be digital. The judgment is not.

An examination begins with authority, scope, and a question worth answering. From there, the work depends on careful acquisition, verification, documentation, interpretation, and the discipline to separate what the evidence shows from what someone hoped it would show.

The Examiner's Bench is built around that reality. It holds practical releases for the workflow, tactile objects for the person doing it, and editions that give the quiet consequence of digital evidence work a physical presence.
