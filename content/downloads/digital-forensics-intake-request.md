A weak intake creates avoidable back-and-forth. An examiner receives a device, but not the question the examination is meant to answer. The legal authority may be somewhere else. The requested date range is unclear. A known passcode exists, but nobody knows who has it.

This template gives a requesting officer or investigator one place to provide the information that shapes a defensible and efficient examination:

- case and requestor information
- authority, scope, and express limitations
- submitted devices and evidence identifiers
- requested services and targeted data
- investigative context and time-sensitive concerns
- handling history, access information, and submission notes
- forensic-unit acceptance and clarification fields

The PDF is ready to print. The DOCX is intentionally editable so a department can add its own routing instructions, approvals, evidence-system fields, legal language, and branding.

This is a starting framework, not a universal policy or legal form. Review it against local law, agency policy, prosecutor expectations, retention requirements, and the actual services your forensic unit provides before operational use.
