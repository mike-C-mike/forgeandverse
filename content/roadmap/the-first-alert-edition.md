## The first alert is not the last answer

A security alert is an invitation to investigate, not a verdict. Anomalies, legitimate changes, noisy detections, and actual threats can all demand attention, but the next step is to establish context, check the evidence, and choose a proportionate response.

The Signal's earlier working line says it plainly: **A signal is only the beginning of an answer.**

## What the editorial study illustrates

The red warning light and screen depict the moment attention shifts to a possible incident. The notebook introduces the human work of triage and judgment after that moment.

The apparent map, status labels, and response checklist are fictional set dressing. They are not a system interface to follow, evidence of a specific breach, a real organization's telemetry, or a completed investigative record.

## What comes next

This artwork was approved for publication as a **screen-only development study**. The original physical-edition suggestion was an 8 x 12 portrait composition; the current landscape illustration is not that manufacturing file. A vector-first print master, small material test, and physical proof review remain future decisions. There is no ordering link.
