## A shelf is not the end of the story

Property and evidence work includes decisions and records long after an item has been safely received and placed in storage. Lawful release, authorized disposition and inventory reconciliation demand care of their own.

A cleared location can mean something went wrong. It can also mean an item reached a properly documented, authorized conclusion. This piece explores the second possibility.

## The visual argument

The vacant shelf is the center of the composition. The fictional record and warm light signal completion rather than abandonment. They are illustrative, not a checklist of required actions or evidence that any particular case was handled.

## The next honest gate
