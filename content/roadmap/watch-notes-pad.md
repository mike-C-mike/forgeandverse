## The Watch is broader than one uniform

The product only works if the person at a dispatch console, technology desk, analyst station, or supervisor office can recognize the same basic moment as the person working in the field.

The common problem is unfinished operational context.

## Five prompts, no miniature bureaucracy

The pad does not try to capture everything that happened during a shift. It asks what matters to the next hour:

- what is happening now;
- what should happen next;
- what is waiting on somebody or something else;
- who needs to be told;
- what cannot be allowed to disappear when the shift ends.

Anything that belongs in an official report, CAD record, ticket, case system, or policy-required log still belongs there.

## The next honest gate

The layout is ready to become paper. The first proof should be used by more than one kind of public-safety role. If the prompts only make sense to one audience, the product is too narrow for The Watch.
