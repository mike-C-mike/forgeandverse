## The phrase already earned its place

The Journal piece began with a technical truth: a hash value matters because it gives another person something to check.

That thought works on a wall only if the design preserves the same restraint. This should not look like hacker décor, a terminal screenshot, or an oversized MD5 joke.

## The first physical question is surface

The production file is intentionally vector-led. That removes the resolution problem and lets the first proof concentrate on the things a screen cannot answer: black density, glare, brass warmth, line weight, and whether the composition still feels quiet when it becomes an object.

## Why the first proof stays small

An 8 x 12 proof is not the intended final presence. It is a cheap way to decide whether the visual language belongs on metal or paper before paying for a larger mounted sample.

If the surface is wrong, making it larger only makes the mistake more expensive.

## The next honest gate

Order one physical proof. Handle it in ordinary office light. If the material supports the piece, the next version can establish final wall scale and mounting.
