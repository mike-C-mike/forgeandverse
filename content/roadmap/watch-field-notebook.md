## The notebook has to survive different jobs

The Watch is intentionally broad. That makes a heavily specialized notebook the wrong answer.

A dispatcher and an officer do not document the same work. A technology specialist and an analyst do not have the same workflow. But each may need to map a timeline, capture a number, sketch a relationship, preserve a handoff, or keep a short-lived problem visible.

## Structure is concentrated at the front

The first dummy uses three structured working pages: shift orientation, timeline, and handoff.

The rest of the book returns to ordinary writing space.

That ratio is deliberate. Forge & Verse should not become the person who designs thirty-six pages of boxes for somebody else's job.

## The next honest gate

Bind one. Write in it for several days. The proof needs to reveal whether the trim, wire, paper, and mixed page treatments disappear into use or keep announcing themselves.
