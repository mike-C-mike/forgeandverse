## The next person is a different person

A difficult call can leave unfinished questions, frustration, grief, or exhaustion. Another call may come before there is time to process any of it.

The people in that next interaction did not create the one before. **The next person deserves a fresh beginning.**

The phrase is not an instruction to pretend the previous call had no impact. It recognizes the effort required to enter the next situation with attention, judgment, and respect for the person actually present.

## An ordinary pause rather than a heroic scene

This piece deliberately avoids confrontation, a dramatic takedown, or flashing lights. A radio, notebook, empty seat, and fading light are enough to suggest the transition between assignments.

The setting is fictional. It is not an authentic police vehicle, equipment inventory, department endorsement, or actual scene.

## The next honest gate
