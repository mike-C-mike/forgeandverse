## Defensive work has an awkward victory condition

A dramatic incident is visible.

A quiet month after patching, segmentation, backup testing, log review, access cleanup, and careful maintenance rarely is.

That asymmetry is the subject.

## No hacker costume

The first composition deliberately avoids the visual shorthand cybersecurity merchandise usually reaches for. There is no hooded figure, glowing lock, skull, green code rain, or warning-red attack map.

The geometry is almost infrastructure rather than illustration.

## The next honest gate

The vector production file is ready for a small material proof. The object has to prove that the quiet design remains intentional on a wall rather than simply looking empty.
