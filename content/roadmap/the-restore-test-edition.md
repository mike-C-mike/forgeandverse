## Backup possession is not recoverability

Having a stored copy is valuable, but by itself it does not tell an administrator whether that copy can be restored into a usable system. Recovery depends on more than a file existing: compatibility, access, configurations, dependencies, people, and the practical ability to resume operations all matter.

The Signal's original working line makes the distinction plain: **A backup is a possibility. A restore is proof.**

## A study about the test, not a record of one

The digital illustration uses a backup device, a checklist, and a screen showing completion. Every test result and mark is fictional; the image is not a screenshot from actual infrastructure and does not show a verified recovery exercise.

The intended subject is the discipline of testing a restore before an incident forces the question.

## The next honest gate

The present artwork is approved as an **editorial development study only**. It is not a vector production master, material proof, or orderable edition. Before creating a physical piece, a separate composition should test the original central line, disciplined geometry, printing legibility, and the degree to which decorative recovery UI distracts from the idea.
