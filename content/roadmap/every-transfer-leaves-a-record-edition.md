## Continuity is made through ordinary work

An item does not maintain its own chain of custody. People do that work, one receipt, release, transfer, temporary examination and return at a time.

The significance of a particular handoff may not be visible for months or years. The record still matters in the moment it is created.

## What this illustration represents

The notebook, bag, clipboard and storage drawer are fictional editorial props. The dates and signature-like strokes are invented marks. Nothing in this scene is a custody document, an agency procedure, or a recommended way to complete one.

The point is not the appearance of the form; it is the continuity of accountable people and documented movement.

## The next honest gate

This is an approved *composition study* for the public studio page. It does not establish an approved wall-edition design, a vector print master, or a manufactured product. If pursued as a physical edition, the visual language and legal-form ambiguity must be reconsidered before material testing.
