## The writing is the center

The edition begins with the full Believe in the Badge piece. The image should support the words without turning the work into a recruitment poster or a collage of familiar symbols.

The challenge is not adding more. It is controlling hierarchy, contrast, and breathing room so the reader can enter the piece from several distances.

## Built for a room

A wall edition changes with scale and surface. Metal can add depth and controlled luminosity, but it can also become loud or reflective. Archival paper can feel intimate and literary, but it may ask for a frame that becomes part of the composition.

The final format must make the work more present, not merely more expensive.

## Current study

Typography, text placement, finish, and mounting are being considered together. The piece earns an edition only when the physical object feels inseparable from the message.
