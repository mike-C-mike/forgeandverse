---
title: "Privacy"
description: "How the Forge & Verse website handles visitor information."
---
The current Forge & Verse site is designed as a static website. It does not require visitor accounts, collect newsletter subscriptions, or operate its own checkout.

At launch, the studio does not intend to collect personal information through the website. Standard server and security logs may still be generated by the hosting provider as part of operating and protecting the site.

If analytics, contact forms, email subscriptions, or outside ordering services are added later, this page should be updated before those features are enabled.
