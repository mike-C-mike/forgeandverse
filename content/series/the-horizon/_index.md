*The Horizon Essays* are an ongoing attempt to explore what it means to live more deliberately.

This is not a search for one philosophy to follow or a finished set of rules for living. It is an intentional exploration of ideas, experiences, traditions, and questions that may help me better understand the person I want to become.

There is no fixed curriculum and no publication schedule. Some questions may lead somewhere unexpected. Others may be abandoned entirely. The point is to give the exploration enough structure to go deeper without making the structure more important than what I learn along the way.
