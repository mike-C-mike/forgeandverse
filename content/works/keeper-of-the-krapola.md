The property room receives the valuable, the dangerous, the fragile, the unpleasant, the unidentified, and the thing someone swore would only be there for a week.

This work is a respectful nod to the professionals who catalog it, secure it, find space for it, account for it, and eventually explain why the paperwork matters.

The joke is affectionate. The responsibility is not.
