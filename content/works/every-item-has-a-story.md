Evidence rooms are full of ordinary objects carrying extraordinary weight.

A sealed envelope, a damaged phone, a handwritten note, or a box placed carefully on a shelf may become part of a much larger story.

The people responsible for those objects preserve more than property. They preserve continuity, accountability, and the ability to answer a simple question years later:

**Is this the same item?**
