---
title: "Material Matters"
description: "A wall edition is not finished when the image file is finished. Scale, surface, edge, light, and mounting complete the work."
---
Forge & Verse treats the physical form as part of the composition.

A piece that asks for luminous color, deep blacks, and a clean floating presence may belong on metal. A quieter work may need archival paper, visible fiber, and a substantial frame. The choice follows the image, the reading distance, and the room where the work will live.
