title: "The First Collection"
description: "A growing body of useful releases, working objects, desk pieces, and wall editions across the four Forge & Verse disciplines."
---

The first Forge & Verse collection starts with four **different professional identities**, not one catalog reproduced for four audiences. Digital forensic examination, property and evidence stewardship, law enforcement, and cybersecurity have their own language, responsibilities, visual culture, and reasons to keep an object.

The sixteen-work initial map includes an open release, working object, desk object, and art study for each profession. Those four formats are the **starting snapshot**, not a permanent rule that every profession must receive the same products or the same number of wall editions.

Not everything below is ready to leave the studio. That is intentional. Finished releases, field proofs, active studies, and work still waiting its turn remain visibly different.
