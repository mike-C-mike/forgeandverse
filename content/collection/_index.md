---
title: "The First Collection"
description: "A growing body of useful releases, working objects, desk pieces, and wall editions across the four Forge & Verse disciplines."
---

The first Forge & Verse collection is being built as four complete rooms rather than a pile of unrelated products.

Each discipline gets four different forms: something useful to download, something small enough to live in the work, a more substantial object for the desk or field, and one piece made to hold the meaning of the profession in a room.

Not everything below is ready to leave the studio. That is intentional. Finished releases, field proofs, active studies, and work still waiting its turn remain visibly different.
