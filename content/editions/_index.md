---
title: "Physical Editions"
description: "Art and writing that earn a physical form through scale, surface, edge, mounting, and the room where the work will live."
---
A physical edition is not a file sent to a different printer.

The object has to add something the screen cannot: presence, texture, distance, light, weight, or a deliberate place in the room. Forge & Verse shows an ordering link only after the physical specification has been tested and approved.
