FORGE & VERSE
DIGITAL FORENSICS INTAKE KIT
Version 1.1 | August 4, 2026

PURPOSE
This kit is designed to help digital forensic units receive clearer, more actionable requests from officers and investigators. It centers the authority, scope, investigative objective, submitted evidence, handling history, targets, and time-sensitive concerns needed to begin well.

CONTENTS
1. digital-forensics-intake-request.pdf
   Print-ready two-page intake form.

2. digital-forensics-intake-request.docx
   Editable version for agency branding, routing, approvals, evidence-system fields, and local language.

3. digital-forensics-intake-requester-guide.pdf
   One-page guide for officers and investigators before submitting a request.

4. digital-forensics-intake-requester-guide.docx
   Editable version for local terminology and approved handling instructions.

5. digital-forensics-intake-implementation-guide.pdf
   Four-page guide for adapting, piloting, publishing, and maintaining the intake workflow.

6. digital-forensics-intake-implementation-guide.docx
   Editable implementation guide for agency-specific notes and training.

7. LICENSE.txt
   Use and adaptation terms.

8. SHA256SUMS.txt
   SHA-256 values for the release files in this package.

QUICK START
1. Review the implementation guide.
2. Adapt the DOCX files to local law, policy, services, records requirements, evidence procedures, and routing.
3. Pilot the form with a small group of requesters and examiners.
4. Publish one approved version in a controlled location.
5. Remove superseded copies and maintain a local revision history.

IMPORTANT
This kit is a starting framework. It does not replace legal review, agency policy, prosecutor requirements, evidence procedures, records obligations, or the judgment of a qualified examiner. Do not place passwords or sensitive credentials on the intake form unless that method is specifically approved by your organization.
