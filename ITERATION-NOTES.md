# Iteration 5: Working Studio Foundation

This round rebuilds the framework around a fuller studio experience rather than adding more placeholder cards.

## Major changes

- Rebuilt the visual system into a cleaner, consolidated stylesheet
- Reduced oversized section-heading behavior and tightened split layouts
- Added a real Work archive with discipline filters and URL-aware browsing
- Rebuilt individual work pages as editorial presentations
- Added visual cover treatments for works without finished artwork
- Added dynamic related work and discipline connections
- Rebuilt the Journal archive and article pages
- Rebuilt the Disciplines page with related archive counts and links
- Reframed On the Anvil as a live workbench rather than a business roadmap
- Added a custom Studio page that remains founder-light and practitioner-grounded
- Added proper site icons, manifest, Open Graph metadata, and a social preview card
- Added privacy, use-and-rights, and disclosure foundations
- Improved navigation behavior, keyboard handling, filtering, focus states, and reduced-motion behavior

## First functioning free release

The Verification Desk Card now includes:

- A 4 × 6 inch printable PNG at 300 DPI
- A 1920 × 1080 desktop wallpaper
- A release page with direct download buttons
- Plain-language use guidance

## Content expansion
