A deliberate split

Design responsibility stays with the studio. Transaction responsibility stays with the fulfiller.

Keeping those roles clear lets Forge & Verse qualify the right manufacturer for each object without becoming a warehouse, shipping operation, or generic merchandise catalog.

Forge & Verse

Owns the work and the specification.

  • Design and production direction
  • Material, size, finish, and construction decisions
  • Physical proof review and approval
  • Accurate product photography and studio notes
  • Quality concerns about whether the object matches the approved work

Production partner

Owns the transaction and fulfillment.

  • Secure checkout and payment
  • Applicable taxes
  • Manufacturing
  • Shipping and order status
  • Returns and transaction-specific customer service

External checkout, by design

You will know where you are ordering before you leave Forge & Verse.

Available product pages identify the specialist production partner and make it clear that checkout opens on that partner's platform. Forge & Verse does not disguise an outside transaction as an internal cart.

That tradeoff is intentional: the goal is to send the approved work to the manufacturer best equipped to make it, not to force every object through one fulfillment system for the sake of a uniform checkout.

Studio proof approved

A badge that has to be earned physically.

Forge & Verse uses this language only after the intended production configuration has been ordered, handled, and reviewed as a real object. A render, mockup, or vendor preview is not a physical proof.

What gets reviewed
  • Print and color behavior
  • Surface and glare
  • Reading distance and usable scale
  • Paper, binding, edge, mount, or construction
  • Packaging and arrival condition where relevant
