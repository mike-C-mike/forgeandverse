Wall editions

The surface carries part of the story.

A finished wall piece is more than a file sent to a printer. Scale, finish, edge treatment, reflected light, and mounting change how the words and image occupy a room.

Some Forge & Verse works may become metal editions. Others may belong on archival paper, acrylic, or another material chosen for the piece. The format follows the work.

Founded from the work

Practitioner made

Forge & Verse was founded by Matthew McBride, a law-enforcement technology and digital-forensics practitioner whose work includes digital evidence, property and evidence operations, technical systems, and direct support for investigations.

The studio does not need a founder portrait to prove the point. The details in the work should do that.
