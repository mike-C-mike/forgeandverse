Tactile by choice

Not every thought wants to begin at a keyboard.

Some people map a problem by hand. They remember the shape of a page, the pressure of a pen, and the moment a difficult idea becomes visible in ink.

Paper does not replace the digital record. It gives thinking, continuity, and reflection a physical place before the work returns to the system.

Founded from the work

Practitioner made

Forge & Verse was founded by Matthew McBride, a law-enforcement technology and digital-forensics practitioner whose work includes digital evidence, property and evidence operations, technical systems, and direct support for investigations.

The studio's point of view comes from direct work with the people, systems, and responsibilities it depicts.
