Where the work stands.

The label on each work tells you what has been done and whether you can use or order it. These status definitions are the same across every professional collection.

01

Development

Not for sale
  • Concept
  • In development
  • Proof ready
  • Field proof available
  • Physical proof in review

Proof ready is not orderable. A prepared test file or sample under review is still development work.

02

Production

  • Approved for production
  • Available — orderable physical edition
  • Released — completed digital work

Approval does not mean availability. Only Available indicates a physical order path; Released indicates a finished downloadable work.

03

Archive

Optional
  • Archived

Reference, not checkout. A retired work may remain documented after it is no longer offered. Not every work enters the archive.

The distinction: Development tests the work. Production identifies what has been approved, what can be ordered, and what can be downloaded. Archive preserves retired work when applicable.
